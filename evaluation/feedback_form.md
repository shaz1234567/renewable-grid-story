# Target-Audience Feedback Form

**Project:** The Renewable Grid, Hour by Hour  
**Researcher:** Shazab Ali  
**Status:** Human testing pending  

Use this form for two separate target-audience sessions. Do not fill responses from memory or on a tester’s behalf. Participation should be voluntary; avoid recording identifying information unless the tester consents.

## Standard session protocol

1. Ask the tester to think aloud while exploring the application.
2. Begin at View 1 without explaining the intended finding.
3. Ask the tester to use playback in View 2.
4. Ask the tester to switch View 3 or View 4 between absolute and normalized scale.
5. Ask the tester to explain View 5’s CV in their own words.
6. Ask the tester whether View 6 supports the claim that a portfolio is always steadier.
7. Record observations and exact quotations only when actually given.
